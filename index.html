How PathFinder works

The grid is a city map. Green is the origin, red is the destination, grey cells are buildings, and amber/orange cells are congested roads that cost more to traverse.

  1. Draw walls and optional traffic weights.
  2. Choose an algorithm and press Visualize.
  3. Use Compare All to see which search explores fewer nodes.
  4. Drop two or more waypoints to open the routing matrix and compute a delivery-style tour.
